## English (default)

**Short description** (max 80):

> Speak or type cellar notes: additions, temperatures and lab analyses per tank.

**Full description** (max 4000):

> CellarLog is a cellar log for winemakers, built for wet hands, gloves and cellars without signal.
>
> SPEAK IT, DON'T TYPE IT
> Tap the big button and say "Tank 12, added 20 grams per hectolitre SO2, temperature 22 degrees". CellarLog turns it into a structured entry on the right tank: the addition, the amount, the unit, the temperature. Check it, correct anything, save. Voice recognition runs on your phone.
>
> LAB ANALYSES IN SECONDS
> The Analysis tab has a field for every common value: free and total SO2, temperature, pH, titratable and volatile acidity, alcohol, Brix, residual sugar, density and volume. Add your own fields for anything else.
>
> SEE EVERY TANK AT A GLANCE
> • Each tank card shows its latest readings: temperature, Brix, free SO2
> • Latest values on every tank, with how long ago they were measured
> • Tap a value for its full history and a chart: follow a fermentation day by day
> • Search tanks by name, grape or vintage
>
> WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
> Cellars rarely have reception. CellarLog keeps your tanks and timelines on the phone, and everything you record, edit or delete offline is saved and uploads by itself when you're back online.
>
> ENGLISH AND GEORGIAN
> Every screen in English or Georgian, switched with one tap.
>
> ALSO IN YOUR BROWSER
> Open cellarlog.vercel.app on any computer and see the same tanks and entries.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> No ads, no tracking. Your data is yours: you can delete single entries, tanks or your whole account at any time.

**What's new** (release notes, max 500):

> First release: voice and typed cellar notes, the Analysis tab, latest values and history per tank, offline mode, English and Georgian.

## App content forms (Policy → App content)

**Privacy policy**: https://cellarlog.vercel.app/privacy

**Ads**: No, the app contains no ads.

**App access**: Login required. Give reviewers a test account (email + password) with a few tanks and entries in it. Create it yourself; don't use your personal account.

**Content rating** (IARC questionnaire): category "Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other".
- Violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling: No.
- The app mentions alcohol only as a measured value in winemaking (no promotion or sale): answer the alcohol question accordingly. Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3 (may become 12+ if Google counts alcohol references).

**Target audience**: 18 and over (a professional winemaking tool). Not designed for children.

**News app**: No. **COVID-19 contact tracing**: No. **Government app**: No. **Financial features**: None. **Health**: No.

**Data safety**:
- Does the app collect or share user data? **Yes, collects; does not share.**
- Is data encrypted in transit? **Yes.**
- Can users request deletion? **Yes**: in-app (Account → Delete my account) and on the web.
  - Delete-account URL for the form: https://cellarlog.vercel.app/privacy#delete
- Data types collected:
  | Type | Collected | Shared | Optional | Purpose |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Personal info → Email address | Yes | No | Required | Account management |
  | App activity → Other user-generated content (tanks, notes, analyses) | Yes | No | Required | App functionality |
  | Audio → Voice or sound recordings | **No**: speech is converted to text on the phone by Google's recognizer; CellarLog never receives audio |  |  |  |
- Location, contacts, photos, files, financial info, health, device IDs: not collected.

**Permissions**: RECORD_AUDIO, only while the user taps record, for voice notes.

## Release steps

1. Create the Play Console developer account (personal, $25, ID verification).
2. Create the app: name CellarLog, default language English (United States), App, Free.
3. Fill in Store listing (texts and graphics above) and App content (forms above).
4. **Closed testing** (required for new personal accounts):
   - Create a track, add **at least 12 testers** (their Google account emails), and upload the first `.aab` by hand.
   - Testers opt in via the link, then keep the app installed for **14 days in a row**.
5. Apply for production access (Dashboard), answer Google's questions about the test, then release to production.
6. Later updates: `npx eas-cli@latest build --profile production --platform android`, then `npx eas-cli@latest submit --platform android`. Submitting needs a Google service-account key.
