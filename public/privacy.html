Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

CellarLog is a cellar log for winemakers: you record notes, additions and lab analyses for your tanks. This policy explains what data the app keeps, why, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Android app and the website at cellarlog.vercel.app.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a password. The password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in service; nobody can read it.
  • What you enter: your tanks (name, volume, grape variety, vintage) and your entries (notes, values such as SO2, pH or temperature, and the time you recorded them).
  • On your phone or browser: a copy of your tanks and entries so the app works without signal, changes waiting to upload, your sign-in and your language choice. They stay on that device.

Voice notes and the microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you tap the record button. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition (Google's service on Android), either on the phone or, if offline English isn't installed, by Google online. CellarLog never records, stores or receives audio. Only the text you choose to save is kept, as part of your entry.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no profiling.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone, except the service providers below that run the app for us.

Service providers

  • Supabase (supabase.com): the database and sign-in service that stores your account, tanks and entries.
  • Vercel (vercel.com): hosts the website version.
  • Google: speech recognition on Android, as described above, and app distribution through Google Play.

Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). In the database, every account can only ever read and change its own tanks and entries.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete single entries and tanks at any time. To delete your whole account with all its tanks and entries:

  • in the app: Account → Delete my account, or
  • without the app: sign in at cellarlog.vercel.app and use Account → Delete my account, or
  • email us at menognishviligiorgi@gmail.com from your account's address and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deletion is permanent and immediate; nothing is kept afterwards except what the service providers keep in short-term backups, which expire on their own.

Children

CellarLog is a professional tool for winemakers and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: menognishviligiorgi@gmail.com.
